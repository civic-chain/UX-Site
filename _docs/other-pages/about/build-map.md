# About Build Map

## Meaning

Trust page for the team: the three founders have equal status, cards read as one system, and illustrations strengthen context without replacing people with portraits.

## Desktop

- Overall assembly: vertical stack - top screen, center divider, team grid, compact contact block.
- Top screen: two-column grid. Left side has small label, heading, and subtitle; right side has a quiet seal/decorative mark that does not compete with the heading.
- Center divider: horizontal rule on left and right, short phrase in the center, small star or seal in code.
- Team grid: three equal columns. Cards have the same width, similar height, and one internal rhythm.
- Founder card: vertical flex/grid - initials circle, name, thin rule, role, bio. The lower zone is reserved for illustration so it does not climb over text.
- Contact block: after the cards, a compact centered horizontal strip: mark on the left, short text in the middle, email button on the right.

## Mobile

- Top screen becomes one column; the right seal either disappears or becomes a small quiet mark under the text.
- Center divider stays compact: short rule, star, and phrase without stretching.
- Team grid becomes a vertical card stack. All cards keep equal status; color must not make Daniel or Michael visually more important than Justin.
- Inside each card, the illustration stays lower-right, smaller and quieter; text always has priority.
- Contact block becomes a vertical card: text above, email button below, no long horizontal composition.

## Code / Icons

- Initials circles, small star/seal, contact mark, divider rules, email button, borders, shadows, spacing.
- Names, roles, bios, and email remain live text.

## Do Not Copy

- Fake portraits.
- Full team cards or the full page as an image.
- Bios, names, email, or generated seal text inside images.
- Strongly different card colors that break team equality.
