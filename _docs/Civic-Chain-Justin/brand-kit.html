Brand Voice

Sound like

  • Direct and specific — name the problem plainly
  • Calm authority — confident without hype
  • Civic, not corporate — plain English, no jargon
  • Evidence-first — cite figures, let them land
  • Short sentences — one idea per line

Avoid

  • Blockchain-first language ("on-chain," "web3")
  • Government bureaucrat tone ("leverage synergies")
  • Startup hype ("disrupting," "revolutionizing")
  • Passive voice and hedged claims
  • Em dashes — use commas or periods instead
