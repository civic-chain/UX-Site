Where the work lives

The record holds while the work is happening.

Many small towns run procurement, vendor payments, and public-facing questions through the same one or two people. Civic-Chain is compensating control — a clearer process, a steadier evidence trail, and a public record that does not wait until after the fact.

What follows is what ships first, where the demo opens, and what the next step looks like.

Roadmap

A narrow launch, on purpose.

The first release is scoped to the part small towns feel first. Broader workflow coverage, deeper automation, and extended settlement are later layers — described as later, not as day-one promises.

Launch

Three views, controlled spend, readable record.

Town, contractor, and public views around the same contract. Vendor spend is controlled through the virtual card, completion is recorded before residual payout, and the public record stays visible without reconstruction.

Next

Broader workflow coverage.

Live spend monitoring, burn-rate alerts, and export-ready reporting extend the working surface as the workflow matures in live use.

Later

Extended settlement and public-input layers.
