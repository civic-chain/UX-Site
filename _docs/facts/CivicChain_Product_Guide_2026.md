# **1\.  Platform Overview**

CivicChain is a SaaS platform built exclusively for municipal government. It replaces the paper-era procurement and payment stack — email proposals, manual check runs, ACH delays, and opaque vendor spend — with a single, blockchain-anchored workflow any town can operate without a dedicated IT team.

The platform runs on five integrated modules. Towns can start with just the Transparency Layer (Pilot tier) and activate additional modules as they grow.

| 1 | Proposal Engine | Structured digital RFP and proposal management routed by Contract Reference Number (CRN). Email-based proposal intake with automatic CRN routing Side-by-side vendor comparison in admin console NLP-assisted proposal extraction from unstructured email Deemed-acceptance workflow (3-business-day default) |
| :---: | :---- | :---- |

| 2 | Geolocation Voting | GPS \+ IP-verified citizen and board voting with confidence scoring anchored to the blockchain. Three-layer geolocation verification (GPS, IP, boundary polygon) Mobile app voting with biometric authentication Live voting dashboard with confidence scores per ballot XRPL-anchored results certificate — immutable after close |
| :---: | :---- | :---- |

| 3 | Smart Contract Management | Service Agreement Records (SARs) anchored on-chain with full budget allocation and spend tracking. SAR generated on contract award with XRPL transaction hash Budget split across card pool, residual reserve, and hold-back Work Completion Record (WCR) submission \+ Rail compliance in one step Dispute handling via Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) — service continues while held |
| :---: | :---- | :---- |

| 4 | Phone Tap Payments | RLUSD-funded virtual cards (Apple Pay / Google Pay) locked to approved merchant categories. Company, Individual, and Equipment card tiers per contract MCC lock enforced in real time at point of tap (\<500ms authorization) Biometric authentication ties card to individual worker — not plastic Every tap is a blockchain event; visible on public citizen portal instantly |
| :---: | :---- | :---- |

| 5 | Public Transparency Layer | Read-only public dashboard showing budget, spending by department, and every transaction — no login required. Daily or weekly bank data ingestion via read-only API or CSV SHA-256 hash anchoring to public blockchain on every transaction Spending breakdown by department vs. annual budget Any citizen can verify any transaction on a public block explorer |
| :---: | :---- | :---- |

# **3\.  Payment Processing Rates**

CivicChain processes payments on the Ripple Payments Rail using RLUSD (1 RLUSD \= 1 USD at all times). Settlement is real-time and immutable on the XRP Ledger. There are no per-transaction fees on the Transparency Pilot tier (read-only). Payment processing fees apply on tiers with active payment modules.

| Provider | Model | Effective Rate | Settlement Time |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| CivicChain | Ripple Rail · RLUSD · Blockchain-anchored | 0.25–0.75% | Minutes |
| Tyler Technologies / NIC | Government payment portals | 2.25–2.75% | 1–3 business days |
| PayIt | Govtech per-transaction | 1.75–2.5% | 1–3 business days |
| Stripe (commercial) | Standard card processing | \~2.9% | 2 business days |
| Legacy ACH / Check | Manual runs | Flat bank fees \+ labor | 3–5 business days |

At $385,000 in annual contract volume (small town, Community tier), switching from NIC’s 2.5% portal rate to CivicChain’s 0.35% saves approximately $8,300 per year in processing fees alone — nearly covering the entire annual platform fee.
