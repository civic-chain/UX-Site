# Open External Blockers

This is the compact owner hub for blockers that cannot be closed from repo
evidence alone. It preserves the raw audit findings as owner-readable decisions,
not as a chronological findings log.

## Founder blockers

- **Traction:** no live customer, LOI, signed pilot, reference town, or named
  champion is recorded; see [[business-status]].
- **Why now:** no founder-owned trigger explains why the status quo becomes
  intolerable this year; [[02-the-problem]] owns the problem framing.
- **Customer evidence:** vendor onboarding effort, vendor legibility, worker
  incentive, resident return-hook, and real town/vendor/resident demand are not
  proven; see [[16-journey-vendor]], [[17-journey-worker]], and
  [[18-journey-resident]].
- **Go-to-market:** municipal-association endorsement is the path of record, but
  the fallback motion if associations decline is still founder-owned; see
  [[09-go-to-market]].
- **Pilot proof:** the pilot KPI, conversion threshold, and sub-$5K pilot to
  board-approved production path are undefined; see [[business-status]].
- **Market / team proof:** market counts are reconciled internally, but external
  citations and source-of-record team bios remain founder-owned before external
  use; see [[04-market-and-tam]] and [[10-team]].

## Counsel blockers

- **Regulated party:** the day-one licensed money-transmitter, BaaS sponsor,
  program manager, and BSA-liability owner are not named.
- **Float:** the model books float yield, but counsel has not confirmed CivicChain
  may retain interest on municipal public funds.
- **IP:** provisional filings exist, but non-provisional conversion, freedom to
  operate, and executed assignment / ownership are unproven.
- **Records:** immutable ledger anchoring lacks a counsel-cleared correction,
  sealing, PII, and vendor-confidentiality model.
- **Procurement and voting:** sub-$5K pilot structure, contract-stacking risk,
  civic input touching vendor selection, open-meeting duties, and election /
  voting-rights exposure are not cleared.

Detailed legal owner: [[legal-and-compliance]].

## Eng / product blockers

- **Seed stack:** Rail-first vs BaaS-first remains the largest v1.0 architecture
  fork; see [[12-roadmap-and-stages]].
- **v1.0 acceptance:** there is no written acceptance test for issuer path,
  decline, logging, ledger coverage, settlement timing, and failure behavior.
- **Performance:** latency, timeout behavior, and load are unmeasured on a real
  USD-bridge path.
- **Demo boundary:** the repo separates demo from production in many places, but
  a first-failure analysis for a real town is still missing.
- **TRIO / export:** CivicChain exports CSV/PDF and does not integrate with Harris
  TRIO; whether compensating controls beat duplicate entry is unproven.
- **Card scope:** demo and product story treat MCC-locked cards as core, while
  the white paper Release 1 says no virtual cards until Release 2.
- **Harpswell spend:** the three itemized demo card transactions sum to
  $9,214.50, while the financial flow uses $13,214.50; the $4,000 gap must be
  reconciled before external use.

## What closes this hub

Founder decisions, counsel memos, or product/engineering measurements close these
items in their owner docs first. This hub then shrinks or disappears; it should
not become a second source of truth.
