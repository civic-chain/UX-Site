## How to read this page

product.md states the discipline plainly in its Team section: "The bio details

conflict with the marketing source. Do not use numeric team claims externally

## The core signal

Three co-founders. The thesis-level signal product.md leads with: they come from
**inside the industry they are disrupting** — incumbent municipal software,
security-cleared government IT delivery, and Fortune-100-scale engineering. This
"from inside" framing is the part product.md presents as the team's positioning;
the supporting numbers underneath it are the part that needs confirmation.

**Founder-credential signal — Needs proof.** The narrative (ex-Tyler product
owner, cleared government programs, Fortune 100 CTO, named open-source
maintainer) is strong and directly load-bearing for fundraising. Treat the
specific figures, titles, company names, and tenure years as **Needs proof**
(founder confirmation) before external use.
