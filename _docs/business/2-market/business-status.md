# Business Status — Traction, Pipeline, Conversion

## Claim Boundary

- Claim status: mixed — most lines are 'none yet' or Needs proof. This doc exists to make that explicit and owned, not to claim traction.
- Source note: [[_docs/facts/product]] (Harpswell = demo, no live customer); [[_docs/business/2-market/05-customers-and-beachhead]] (8 target towns, Target); [[_docs/business/3-model/09-go-to-market]] (the motion). The status facts themselves are founder-owned and mostly not yet in the canon.

A **living status document**, not part of the numbered pitch narrative. It exists
because the audit kept hitting questions — live customer? named champion? pilot
metric? — that had no home in the canon and went silently unanswered. Here they
are answered honestly, even when the honest answer is "none yet."
[[05-customers-and-beachhead]] points here for live-customer confirmation.

## Customers and commitments — as of 2026-06-03

- **Live customers:** none. **Actual** — product.md states Harpswell is a demo
  scenario, not a confirmed live customer.
- **Signed pilots:** none recorded. **Needs founder input** if one exists.
- **LOIs / design partners:** none recorded. **Needs founder input.**
- **Reference customer:** none. The single most valuable missing GTM artifact.

## Pipeline — the eight target towns

The eight priority Maine towns in [[05-customers-and-beachhead]] are a **Target**
profile-fit list. For each, the following is **Needs founder input** and not yet
known:

- a **named internal champion**,
- evidence of **budget**, **urgency**, and exercised **authority**.

A list of towns that fit a profile is not a pipeline until these exist.

## Pilot success metric

**Undefined — Needs founder input.** No single pilot KPI with a target threshold
exists. Candidate signals (all **Modeled**): bid-premium reduction (~5% in
[[05-customers-and-beachhead]]), days-to-payment, audit / FOIA hours. One must be
chosen as the proof that the wedge works.

## Pilot to production conversion

**Unproven.** The wedge is a sub-$5K pilot a Town Manager can buy alone (Maine
30-A §2636), but production vendor payments require a select-board warrant
(30-A §5603, ~2–4 week cycle — see
[[13-risks-and-mitigations#Maine-specific constraints]]). No evidence yet that a
sub-threshold pilot becomes a budgeted, board-approved production line.
**Needs founder input:** any such conversion precedent.

## What removes these gaps

Founder-supplied: one real reference town with its dollar pain, a named champion
per pursued town, a defined pilot metric and threshold, and a documented pilot to
production conversion. Until then, every line above stays honest about being
empty.
